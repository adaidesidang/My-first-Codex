Update Notes

Version Log

Version 3.1

May 31, 2026

Polished Local Versus setup screens, improved role random animation, refreshed the map preview, and fixed several small visual bugs.

Version 3.0

May 31, 2026

Added Local Versus, a same-device two-player mode with role select, skin select, and customizable map rules.

P1 P2

Player 1 uses WASD and Q. Player 2 uses arrow keys and / or ?. Versus scores are kept out of leaderboard.

Version 2.3

May 31, 2026

Added a smoother animated leaderboard switch, French-styled level labels, and sharper AI Pac routing in Duo and Trios Ghost maps.

Ghost Mode now gives AI Pac one Power Bean in every map size, keeping the hunt fair but active.

Version 2.2

May 31, 2026

Added Ghost Skin selection and a new wormhole anti-blocking rule for Ghost Mode. If the player ghost holds one tile too long, it is pulled into a deep-blue portal.

Flash scoreboard animation now lasts longer, and portals move the player ghost to the hole farthest from Pac with a short mist-and-particle teleport effect.

Version 2.1

May 31, 2026

Tuned Ghost Mode pacing with a clearer countdown, Pac-first bean chasing, and two-page leaderboards for Pac and Ghost records.

Flash Bean now belongs only to the player ghost, dashes four tiles with a color afterimage, and shows a small golden lightning mark during the dash.

Version 2.0

May 31, 2026

Added Ghost Mode, a new role where the player controls the lead ghost and hunts an AI Pac. The Pac still focuses on clearing beans, but uses cautious pathing when a ghost gets too close.

Added Flash Bean, a ghost-only item for the player ghost. It uses the same X / center-button input as Power Bean, but turns that input into a short directional dash.

Version 1.2

May 31, 2026

Improved three-ghost chase behavior. Ghosts now hold straight-line pursuit more consistently without increasing their movement speed.

Added a small pressure-balance adjustment for rare close-chase situations, plus minor gameplay refinements.

Version 1.1

May 31, 2026

Power phase time now scales by difficulty: two-ghost mode lasts 7.5s, and three-ghost mode lasts 8s.

One-ghost maps now have a 50 percent chance to include one power bean. Scoreboard power tracking, device selection, and small gameplay details were polished.

Version 1.0

May 31, 2026

A night-blue power bean now appears in harder mazes. Eat it, then press X on desktop or tap the center mobile button to phase a nearby ghost.

The beam checks up to seven open tiles in the four straight directions. Walls block it. A phased ghost turns misty, slows down, and drifts for seven seconds.

Two-ghost maps can hold 1-2 beans. Three-ghost maps can hold 2-3 beans.
