Version Log

Version 1.1

May 31, 2026

Power phase time now scales by difficulty: two-ghost mode lasts 7.5s, and three-ghost mode lasts 8s.

One-ghost maps now have a 50 percent chance to include one power bean. Scoreboard power tracking, device selection, and small gameplay details were polished.

Version 1.0

May 31, 2026

A night-blue power bean now appears in harder mazes. Eat it, then press X on desktop or tap the center mobile button to phase a nearby ghost.

The beam checks up to seven open tiles in the four straight directions. Walls block it. A phased ghost turns misty, slows down, and drifts for seven seconds.

Two-ghost maps can hold 1-2 beans. Three-ghost maps can hold 2-3 beans.
